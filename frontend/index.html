A valid fertility prediction is required.

🔍 Why these recommendations?

The system uses the predicted soil fertility together with pH, moisture, temperature, nitrogen and CEC to evaluate crop suitability. Each recommendation includes a reason explaining why the crop is suitable under the current conditions.
